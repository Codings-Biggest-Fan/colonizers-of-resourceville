# M03 board surface brief

Mode: native Operate/Experience. Job: inspect a reproducible base island and its topology. No playable game controls. Existing product scope and Catppuccin direction are pinned by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a legible cartoon island carries the experience through imagery, with no permanent chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Catppuccin Mocha native chrome, blue focus, simplified terrain silhouettes, cream number discs, cargo boats and clean blue sea.

STORY: immediately recognize the resource island, inspect a tile or port, then adjust layout or camera without losing context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: island centered in light blue sea filling the window; no top/bottom bars, Board label or zoom slider. Wheel zoom and right-drag pan; setup/fit live in a context menu. No labels on terrain or ports, no fabricated hand or score.

FORM: extend M03's native board surface per direct user corrections; seed: none (unavailable). No web runtime installed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Exit review: inspect final native renders at default, compact, wide and scaling stress; verify actual input and camera behavior; archive results. Source checks and generated asset inspection alone do not complete that review.
